# Goldfish

A tiny goldfish in a white tank. Feed it, lead it around, play with it, wipe the glass and turn the lights off. Made for iPhone, works on a laptop too.

**Play:** https://michellejnoh.github.io/goldfish/

## How to play

- **hand**: drag to lead the fish, tap the water to call it, tap the fish to say hi (double-tap for a trick)
- **food**: tap to sprinkle flakes
- **wash**: wipe the smudges off the glass
- **toy**: drag the lure and the fish chases it
- **sleep**: lights off; the fish rests and gets its energy back

Its needs (full, clean, energy, fun) drop in real time, even while the page is closed. Everything is saved only in your own browser; the site collects nothing.
